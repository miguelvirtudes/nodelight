NodeLight Manual Support Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

NodeLight is made by Miguel Gonçalves. This policy explains what the app does with your data. In short: NodeLight does not collect any personal data.

Data we collect

None. NodeLight has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send any information to the developer or to any third party.

Local network

NodeLight asks for access to your local network. It uses it only to send lighting data (Art-Net DMX) to lighting nodes on your Wi-Fi network, to find those nodes, and to share the lighting state with other phones running NodeLight on the same network. This traffic stays on your local network and never goes to the internet.

Files on your device

Your shows are stored as files on your iPhone, in the NodeLight folder of the Files app. They are included in your device backups like any other app data. They leave your device only when you choose to export or share them.

Purchases

The Full Version is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; the developer does not receive your payment details or your name. NodeLight only checks with the App Store whether the Full Version was bought on your Apple Account.

Children

NodeLight does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
